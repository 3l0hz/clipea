# **App Name**: elohz

## Core Features:

- Premium Product Catalog Display: Showcase product cards on the homepage and category pages, featuring images, names, categories, and prices within a dark, premium, and minimal design, incorporating subtle glow effects for visual appeal.
- Advanced Product Detail Modal: Implement a responsive and premium modal for detailed product viewing, supporting a main image, additional thumbnails, optional 3D models (GLB/GLTF), and video (MP4/WEBM). Includes product name, brand, price, description, compatibility, and recommended use. Optimized with distinct layouts for mobile and desktop.
- Direct WhatsApp Integration for Sales: Enable instant purchasing and inquiries via WhatsApp, with pre-filled messages including product names. A floating WhatsApp button provides easy access to customer support.
- Dynamic Product Category Filtering: Allow users to efficiently filter product listings by specific categories such as Selfie Sticks, Soportes Moto, Trípodes, and Accesorios Cámara, ensuring a tailored browsing experience.
- Mobile-First Responsive Layouts: Ensure the entire store provides a seamless and optimized experience across all devices, prioritizing mobile design for navigation (minimalist drawer menu), product display (2 items per row), and detailed views (centered modals).
- Configurable Homepage & Navigation Structure: Develop a premium homepage featuring a sticky minimalist header, a dark technological hero section, benefits bar, main categories, featured products, a dedicated 'Graba mejor tus rutas y aventuras' section, FAQ, and a simple footer with contact details.

## Style Guidelines:

- Backgrounds: A very dark gray/black (#0B0B0B) serving as the main background, contrasted with a slightly lighter dark gray (#111111) for cards and prominent content blocks, establishing a deep and modern visual foundation.
- Primary Color & Text: Crisp white (#FFFFFF) is used for all main text and as the background for primary call-to-action buttons, ensuring maximum readability and emphasis against the dark theme. Secondary text is a softer light gray (#A1A1A1).
- Borders & Secondary Buttons: Subtle dark gray borders (#1F1F1F) define sections and elements. Secondary buttons are transparent with a white border, maintaining a minimalist and cohesive aesthetic.
- Accent Color: A vibrant neon green (#00FF9C) is strategically applied for minimal details, highlights, and subtle calls-to-action, adding a touch of technological and energetic flair to the dark palette.
- Headline Font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) is chosen for all headlines to convey a modern, clean, and technological aesthetic with a strong visual hierarchy. This font is excellent for larger titles.
- Body Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) will be used for all body text, ensuring high legibility and a neutral, professional appearance across both mobile and desktop interfaces, complementing the overall minimalist design.
- Icons: Utilize a set of minimalist, clean, and clear line-art icons that complement the dark, premium, and technological aesthetic. Icons should be easily understandable and contribute to an uncluttered interface.
- Mobile-Specific Layout: Products displayed two per row, a centrally positioned and animating product detail modal with a blurred background, and a minimalist drawer menu for streamlined navigation.
- Desktop Layout: Product cards arranged in three or four columns. Product detail modals will adopt a two-column structure (image gallery on the left, information on the right), and a sticky, minimalist header will be used.
- Smooth Transitions: Subtle and fast transitions (0.2s to 0.3s) for all interactive UI elements. Product cards will feature a subtle scale animation on touch for mobile and a smooth hover effect on desktop, providing premium tactile feedback.
- Optimized Performance: Animations and asset loading are strictly optimized for mobile performance, including lazy-loading non-critical images, preventing video auto-play, and using video posters to ensure a fast and fluid user experience.
